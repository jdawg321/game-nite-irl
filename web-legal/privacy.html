Game Nite Privacy Policy

Draft for the TestFlight beta · Last updated November 23, 2026

Game Nite runs real-world group games like Sardines. This policy explains what the app collects during the beta, why, and how long it is kept.

What we collect

  • Account: your sign-in identifier from Sign in with Apple or your email address, and a display name you choose.
  • Age confirmation: that you confirmed you are 18 or older, and that you agreed to the safety rules.
  • Game activity: games you join, your role, readiness and results.
  • Location during games: only while you are in an active game, the app samples your location every 20–30 seconds with its accuracy. It is used to verify finds, enforce the game boundary, compute rankings and keep players safe. Other players only see locations the game rules allow, and never while a game is paused or over.
  • Replays (optional): if you turn on replay consent, organizers can view your path after a game. Replays are deleted 7 days after the game.
  • Safety reports: incident reports you submit.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We don't track your location outside an active game.
  • We don't use third-party analytics or ad SDKs.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in the United States. Access is restricted by row-level security so each player and organizer only sees what they need.

Retention and deletion

Raw location samples are kept only as long as needed to run and review a game. Replays are deleted after 7 days. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. You can also email us.
